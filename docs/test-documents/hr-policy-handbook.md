# Contoso Corporation Employee Handbook

## Chapter 1: Introduction

Welcome to Contoso Corporation! This handbook outlines our company policies, benefits, and workplace guidelines. All employees are expected to familiarize themselves with these policies.

## Chapter 2: Time Off and Leave Policies

### 2.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)

Contoso provides generous paid time off to support work-life balance:

- **New employees (0-2 years)**: 15 days PTO per year
- **Mid-level employees (2-5 years)**: 20 days PTO per year  
- **Senior employees (5+ years)**: 25 days PTO per year

PTO accrues monthly and unused days can be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days. Employees must submit PTO requests at least 2 weeks in advance for requests of 3+ days.

### 2.2 Sick Leave

All employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over and resets on January 1st. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required.

### 2.3 Parental Leave

Contoso offers comprehensive parental leave:
- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks paid leave
- **Secondary caregiver**: 6 weeks paid leave
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption

### 2.4 Bereavement Leave

Employees may take paid bereavement leave:
- Immediate family member: 5 days
- Extended family member: 3 days

## Chapter 3: Remote Work Policy

### 3.1 Hybrid Work Model

Contoso operates on a hybrid work model. Employees are expected to be in the office a minimum of 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday are designated office days). Remote work is permitted for the remaining days.

### 3.2 Remote Work Requirements

When working remotely, employees must:
- Maintain a dedicated workspace with reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps)
- Be available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Use company-approved VPN for all work activities
- Attend all scheduled meetings via video with camera enabled

### 3.3 Equipment

The company provides:
- Laptop computer
- Monitor (one)
- Keyboard and mouse
- $500 home office stipend (one-time)

## Chapter 4: Compensation and Benefits

### 4.1 Pay Schedule

Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. Direct deposit is required for all employees.

### 4.2 Health Insurance

Contoso offers three health insurance plans:
1. **Basic Plan**: $50/month employee contribution, $1,500 deductible
2. **Standard Plan**: $150/month employee contribution, $750 deductible
3. **Premium Plan**: $300/month employee contribution, $250 deductible

All plans include dental and vision coverage. Coverage begins on the first day of employment.

### 4.3 401(k) Retirement Plan

- Company matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of salary
- Additional 50% match on next 2% of salary
- Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested after 4 years

### 4.4 Professional Development

Each employee receives a $2,500 annual professional development budget for:
- Conferences and workshops
- Online courses and certifications
- Books and learning materials

## Chapter 5: Code of Conduct

### 5.1 Workplace Behavior

All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Report any harassment or discrimination immediately
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

### 5.2 Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager and HR. This includes:
- Financial interests in competitors or vendors
- Outside employment that may conflict with Contoso duties
- Personal relationships with subordinates or vendors

### 5.3 Social Media Policy

When posting about Contoso on social media:
- Clearly state opinions are your own
- Do not share confidential information
- Do not disparage colleagues, customers, or competitors

## Chapter 6: Performance Management

### 6.1 Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice yearly:
- **Mid-year review**: June (feedback and goal alignment)
- **Annual review**: December (comprehensive evaluation and compensation review)

### 6.2 Performance Ratings

Employees are rated on a 5-point scale:
1. Does Not Meet Expectations
2. Partially Meets Expectations
3. Meets Expectations
4. Exceeds Expectations
5. Significantly Exceeds Expectations

### 6.3 Promotion Process

Promotions are considered during annual reviews based on:
- Consistent performance at "Exceeds Expectations" level
- Demonstration of next-level competencies
- Business need and headcount availability
